Privacy policy

Version 1.0 · Effective 7 November 2026

In short: VidNest has no accounts and no VidNest server that stores your links, downloads or library. Everything you save stays on your phone. The only outside party that receives data from the app itself is the advertising network that shows the ads.

What stays on your phone

Links you paste, the videos and audio you download, your library, your queue and your settings are stored on your device only. We do not receive them. Videos are saved in the Movies folder of your phone and audio in the Music folder, each inside a VidNest folder. Uninstalling VidNest removes the app's settings, and files you downloaded stay until you delete them.

What VidNest asks your phone for

  • Internet: to fetch the video or audio from the link you paste, and to load ads.
  • Notifications: optional. Shows downloads, conversions and the song that is playing while you are in another app.
  • Background activity: lets downloads and playback continue while VidNest is minimised.
  • Clipboard: when VidNest opens and your clipboard holds a supported video link, it offers to download it. The check happens on your phone, the clipboard is never sent to us, and you can turn it off in Permissions and privacy inside the app.
  • Audio and video files: optional. Requested only when you open Library, then Local, so VidNest can list media already on your phone.

Support info (optional)

If a download fails, the info screen in VidNest has a Copy support info button. It puts a short technical report on your clipboard: the VidNest version, your phone model, Android version and processor, whether the download components started, and the last error. Nothing is sent anywhere when you tap it. We only receive that text if you paste it into a message and send it to us yourself.

Links you download from

When you download a link, your phone connects directly to the site that hosts it (for example YouTube, Instagram or Facebook), the same way a browser would. That site sees your IP address and the request, and its own privacy policy applies. VidNest may also fetch updates to its download components in the background.

Ads

VidNest is free and shows ads through Unity Ads, a third-party advertising network. To choose, deliver and measure ads it may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is done by that network under its own policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings under Privacy, then Ads. Watching one rewarded ad pauses interstitial ads for 30 minutes.

Children

VidNest is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Change or withdraw any permission at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Turn clipboard link detection off inside the app.
  • Delete your downloads from Library or from the VidNest folders in Movies and Music.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
